# 3. Compare rates and baskets

*A good rate answers the price per unit. It does not automatically choose the whole purchase.*

The sauce recipe uses 3/4 cup for three people. Dividing its amount by 3 gives 1/4 cup per person. At the same portion size, five people need 5×1/4 = 5/4 = 1 1/4 cups. Check by dividing 5/4 cup by five people: the original per-person rate returns. Adding two people and two cups does not preserve that rate. For a two-person increase, add two portions, or 1/2 cup.

Now shop for a different ingredient sold by weight. The sauce is measured by volume and the purchased staple by weight; we make no conversion between cups and ounces.

## Unit price is one comparison

| Package | Size | Price | Price per ounce |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small | 6 ounces | $1.50 | $0.25 |
| Large | 10 ounces | $2.00 | $0.20 |

Divide cents by ounces to avoid an ambiguous decimal: 150 cents / 6 oz = 25 cents per oz. Multiply the rate by the package size to recover 150 cents. The large package is cheaper per ounce. The task, however, is to get at least 12 ounces in whole packages, spending as little as possible, with no resale or credit for leftovers.

| Two-package basket | Ounces | Total price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Two small | 12 | $3.00 |
| One small, one large | 16 | $3.50 |
| Two large | 20 | $4.00 |

No single package reaches 12 ounces. Any basket of three or more costs at least 3×$1.50 = $4.50, more than two small packages. Those cases exhaust the possibilities, so two small is the unique cheapest feasible basket. If buying for several meals, the extra ounces could be useful; that is a different requirement. State the quantity you want before looking at the shelf tag.

## Read the directions of the units

Ounces per dollar is the inverse of dollars per ounce. It answers a different question. Higher ounces per dollar means better unit value; lower dollars per ounce means better unit value. Forgetting which metric you wrote can reverse the ranking even when the quotients are correct.

**Try it unaided.** How much sauce for seven people at the same portion size? For a new need of at least 16 ounces of the staple, redo the basket comparison and show that no one-package or three-package choice can beat your result.
