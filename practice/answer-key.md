# Answer key and scoring

## Chapter checks

1. Balances: −$4, $11, $5, $11. Reversing the −$6 charge moves right $6. Removing the original deposit from the final $11 recovers −$4.
2. 7/20 = 35/100 = 0.35 = 35%. Taking that fraction of $20 gives $7. The fraction is a proportion of a reference whole; the result inherits the dollar unit.
3. At 1/4 cup per person, seven need 7/4 = 1 3/4 cups. For a 16-oz minimum, two small fail; a mixed basket costs $3.50 and meets it; two large cost $4.00. One package cannot meet it, and at least three cost at least $4.50.
4. The mixed basket leaves $25.00. Solve 25.00−s=18.00 to get $7.00. Spending $7.01 leaves $17.99, one cent short.

## Transfer

The ledger leaves −3+27−9 = $15.00. Sauce rate is (2/3)/4 = 1/6 cup per serving, so six servings use one cup. One small package is insufficient. One large meets the requirement at $2.10, and any basket of two or more costs at least $2.40. The result does not carry to every required quantity. The post-purchase balance is $12.90. Solving 12.90−s=10.00 gives $2.90, and substitution leaves exactly $10.00.

## Suggested scoring

Use 0 for missing or incorrect, 1 for partial, and 2 for clearly supported on each dimension: signed ledger and reverse check; equivalent forms and recipe rate; feasible basket and minimality; equation, units and substitution; chart comparison; verified revision with assistance disclosed; and unaided transfer. Count a correct method even when its notation differs from this key.
